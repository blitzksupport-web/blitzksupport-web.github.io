Privacy Policy

WW2 Event Clicker

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BLITZ K handles information associated with WW2 Event Clicker, including locally saved gameplay data, optional analytics, and optional rewarded advertisements.

Developer: BLITZ K
Contact: blitzk.support@gmail.com

1. Introduction

WW2 Event Clicker is a historical idle and clicker game developed by BLITZ K.

We aim to respect player privacy, minimize unnecessary data collection, and provide clear information about the services used by our game.

This policy applies to WW2 Event Clicker on supported mobile platforms.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

WW2 Event Clicker stores gameplay information locally on your device so your progress can be preserved between sessions.

This information may include:

  • Campaign progress and unlocked historical events.
  • Supplies, War Tokens, and Blitz balances.
  • Player ranks, promotions, and statistics.
  • Upgrades, boosts, and mission progress.
  • Game preferences and settings.
  • Offline earnings and reward records.
  • Local data used to maintain save integrity.

The game does not currently require players to register an online gameplay account.

Locally stored gameplay information is not ordinarily transmitted to a BLITZ K account server.

3. Optional Analytics

WW2 Event Clicker integrates Unity Analytics to help us understand gameplay and improve the experience for players.

Analytics collection is optional and is controlled separately from advertising preferences.

When enabled, analytics information may include:

  • Gameplay sessions and session activity.
  • Historical chapter progression.
  • In-game purchases made using virtual currency.
  • Mission completion and promotion events.
  • Rewarded-ad interaction events.
  • App installation identifiers.
  • Technical device and application information.
  • IP address and related technical information processed by Unity.

This information is used to understand player progression, investigate gameplay issues, evaluate game balance, and improve the game.

Players can decline analytics collection and continue playing.

Analytics preferences can be changed through the game's privacy settings.

4. Optional Rewarded Advertisements

WW2 Event Clicker is designed without mandatory advertising interruptions.

Players may voluntarily choose to watch rewarded advertisements to receive optional gameplay benefits.

The game integrates Unity LevelPlay, with ironSource Ads and Unity Ads as advertising network partners.

When advertising functionality is enabled, these services may process information including:

  • Device advertising identifiers.
  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it.
  • Device model and operating system.
  • Application and advertising interaction data.
  • Advertisement views and reward events.
  • Technical data used for security and fraud prevention.

Advertising providers may use this information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and other purposes described in their own privacy policies.

Advertising consent and privacy choices are separate from Unity Analytics preferences. Where required, appropriate advertising consent mechanisms will apply.

Declining an optional rewarded advertisement does not prevent ordinary gameplay.

5. Third-Party Services

WW2 Event Clicker uses or integrates third-party technologies.

These include:

  • Unity Analytics: Optional gameplay analytics.
  • Unity LevelPlay: Advertising mediation for optional rewarded advertisements.
  • ironSource Ads: Advertising network services.
  • Unity Ads: Advertising network services.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

You can review Unity's privacy information here:

Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy

6. Data Retention

Locally stored gameplay information generally remains on the device until it is deleted through the game's available controls, removed through device storage management, or removed when the application is uninstalled.

Information processed by Unity Analytics or advertising providers may be retained according to their respective retention policies and legal obligations.

BLITZ K does not control every retention period applied by independent third-party providers.

7. Deleting Your Data

WW2 Event Clicker provides an option in Settings to delete locally stored player data.

This removes locally saved campaign progression, in-game currencies, player statistics, upgrades, and other associated gameplay records.

Deleting local gameplay data does not automatically erase information that third-party services may already have processed.

Players can separately disable Unity Analytics collection and request deletion of associated analytics information through the game's privacy controls.

If an analytics deletion request cannot be transmitted while offline, the game is designed to retry that request when connectivity returns.

For additional privacy or deletion inquiries, contact BLITZ K using the email address below.

8. Data Security

We aim to minimize unnecessary collection of player information.

Third-party providers maintain their own security measures for information processed through their services.

Although reasonable security practices are important, no electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

WW2 Event Clicker is not specifically directed toward children.

The game contains educational and historical material concerning World War II.

The game does not currently request personal information through an online gameplay account registration system.

Applicable child-privacy and advertising requirements will be followed according to relevant laws and platform policies.

10. International Data Processing

Third-party service providers may process information in countries outside the player's country of residence.

Such processing is subject to applicable legal requirements and the providers' privacy practices.

11. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain processing.

Available in-game controls include analytics preferences and deletion of locally stored gameplay data.

Additional requests may be directed to our support email.

12. Contacting BLITZ K

If you contact BLITZ K by email, we may receive your email address and the information you voluntarily provide.

This information is used to respond to support requests, feedback, and privacy inquiries. Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably needed for those purposes and applicable legal obligations.
